About us

Aiming to be a leading energy supplier in Africa

Sno Queens is an independent power producer based in Durban, South Africa. We focus on environmentally conscious energy solutions and are committed to developing renewable energy projects.

  • Ownership100% Black female-owned
  • Established2017
  • Based inDurban, South Africa
  • FocusSolar, gas, project management

Mission

To advance the energy transformation agenda through the optimal use of energy resources in power generation, transportation fuels, industrial and just energy transition sectors.

Vision

To be a leading independent energy supplier, developing clean renewable energy projects in emerging markets and empowering developing-country populations with sustainable power.

Our focus areas

Solar PV

Renewable power plants that generate electricity from solar energy using solar panels. We also supply solar panels.

Gas-to-Power

Natural gas used as a fuel source to generate electricity, a reliable and efficient way to produce power. We also transport oil and gas to clients.

Project Management Office

Governance, performance tracking and stakeholder alignment, so every energy project we take on is delivered with consistency, transparency and excellence.
